## Featured project

### Longitudinal evaluation of TIMP concentrations in a latent TB population

ICMR-National Institute for Research in Tuberculosis, Chennai · May to July 2026

I compared circulating TIMP-1, TIMP-2, TIMP-3 and TIMP-4 between people with latent TB infection (LTBI) and healthy controls, at baseline and at a six-month follow-up.

| Group | Baseline | Follow-up (6 months) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| LTBI-positive | 43 | 24 |
| Healthy controls | 43 | 24 |

- Classified participants with the QuantiFERON-TB Gold Plus interferon-gamma release assay (IGRA).
- Measured the four TIMPs with a Luminex multiplex assay.
- Compared groups with the Mann-Whitney U test in GraphPad Prism 9.2.0.
- TIMP-2 was the only marker that differed significantly between the groups (p < 0.05), at both time points. TIMP-1, TIMP-3 and TIMP-4 did not (p > 0.05).

**Methods:** `IGRA` `ELISA` `Luminex` `GraphPad Prism`
